Sarasota’s ‘Freezing’ Winter

Is Sarasota’s winter really freezing?

Sarasota residents sometimes describe winter days as freezing. But “cold” depends on what your body is used to. For someone from Mongolia, a Sarasota winter day can look much more like a mild or even summery day at home.

The takeaway

The same temperature that feels painfully cold to a Sarasota resident can be ordinary warm-season weather in Mongolia. A Sarasota winter day can feel like a break from the cold to someone who grew up in Ulaanbaatar.
